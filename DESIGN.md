# Design system

## World

The Seafudz executive administration and inventory management interface provides a modern, high-precision operations command workspace. It pairs clean, airy slate grounds with crisp white cards, refined typographic rhythm, and an energetic, warm citrus-orange brand signature that echoes fresh coastal seafood.

## Palette

- Background ground: Slate 50 (`#F8FAFC`) for the consistent executive workspace background.
- Working surfaces: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, modal dialogs, and segmented pill backgrounds.
- Structural borders: Slate 100/200 (`#F1F5F9` / `#E2E8F0`) for whisper-quiet borders.
- Primary text: Slate 900 (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast, confident headings.
- Body & secondary text: Slate 600 (`#475569`) and Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`).
- Brand Accent: Modern Orange (`#F97316` / `orange-500`), hover (`#EA580C` / `orange-600`), and soft wash (`bg-orange-50 text-orange-700`) without heavy opacity or muddy fills.
- Food Category: Warm Orange (`bg-orange-50 text-orange-700`).
- Packaging Category: Crisp Indigo (`bg-indigo-50 text-indigo-700`).
- Optimal / In Stock: Emerald (`#10B981` / `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700`).
- Low Stock / Attention: Amber (`#F59E0B` / `amber-500`).
- Depleted / Critical: Rose (`#EF4444` / `rose-500`).

Status color is always paired with a legible label, badge, or tabular data. Text and controls maintain accessible contrast against their surfaces.

## Typography

Use clean system sans with proportional tracking, clear weight contrast, and tabular figures for numbers, quantities, and Philippine Peso currency (`₱`).

## Composition & Scaffolding

- Header rows: Clean title, subtitle, breadcrumb / location chip, and interactive utility actions.
- Navigation: Modern segmented pill controls with smooth transitions and active white shadows.
- Metric cards: Uniform card padding, muted uppercase or small-caps metric labels, bold tabular numbers, and contextual delta/status subtext.
- Panels: Independent data grids with responsive columns and clean scrollable content areas.
